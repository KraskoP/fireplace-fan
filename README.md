## Purpose
I have in my home fireplace with the external fan to push air into the fireplace. This solution replaces the idea, that the air to the fireplace gets to the fireplace passively. Now I have a fan and I can push the air what makes the burning process more effective, the flames are bigger, ease of initiate the fire.

## Background
I had a manual switch to turn on and off the ventilator and to change the speed. That was uneffective and tedious. Sometimes I forgot to switch off and the fan spins all night long.


## Solution
I found this project:
https://www.pcbway.com/project/shareproject/3_speed_Fan_controller_with_Local_control_f898af42.html

And I decided to connect my ventilator into my Home Assistant. This gives me freedom:
1. I can turn on and off and change gears remotely
2. I created automations allowing me to turn off the fan for the night
